About

A physicist drawn to the invisible Universe.

I am a Spanish astrophysicist working across observational cosmology, galaxy formation, and scientific computing. I am currently based at the Institut de Física d'Altes Energies in Barcelona.

I grew up in Cáceres, a UNESCO World Heritage city in Extremadura. That path eventually took me through Salamanca, Madrid, Aragón, Chicago, Donostia, and Barcelona—each place adding a different perspective to how I approach science and collaboration.

My research asks how visible tracers—galaxies, quasars, and absorption by intergalactic hydrogen—can reveal the dark matter, gas, and physical laws shaping cosmic structure. I combine survey data with simulations and machine-learning models, especially when conventional calculations become too expensive.
