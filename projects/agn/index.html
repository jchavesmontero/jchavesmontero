Lyman-alpha forest

Quasar light carries a three-dimensional map of intergalactic hydrogen. I build fast, physically informed models that turn this forest of absorption into precision measurements of cosmic structure.

DESIHydrodynamical simulationsEmulationNonlinear clustering

The Lyman-alpha forest probes matter across redshifts and scales that are difficult to reach with galaxies. Its cosmological power comes with a modelling challenge: the signal depends on nonlinear gravity, gas physics, thermal history, and observational effects.

My work replaces repeated, expensive simulation campaigns with surrogate models trained on suites of hydrodynamical simulations. These models connect one- and three-dimensional forest statistics, remain sensitive to cosmology and intergalactic-medium physics, and make previously inaccessible scales usable for inference.

Core questionHow much cosmological information can we recover from the nonlinear forest?
